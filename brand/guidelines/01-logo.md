# Logo

The logo is the wordmark **bunk3d**, set in Unbounded ExtraBold, all lowercase, with the **3** picked out. The 3 is the whole identity in one character: it’s the e that got bunked. On its own, the 3 is our app icon and avatar mark.

## Versions

Pick the file for the ground you are on. Never recolour a file.

| File | Use on | The 3 |

## Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** keep a margin equal to the height of the 3 on every side of the wordmark. Nothing else goes in it: no stickers, no edges, no other logos.
- **Minimum size:** the wordmark is 96px wide on screen and 24mm wide in print. Below that, use the app icon.
- **App icon minimum:** 32px on screen, 8mm in print. Keep the corner radius at 22.6% of the side (232 on 1024).
- **Placement:** on posts, stories and posters the wordmark sits bottom-left or top-left, `space-12` in from the edges at 1080px wide. It is never centred over a photo’s subject.

## Never

- Write it as BUNK3D, Bunk3D or Bunked in the logo, or set it in any other typeface.
- Tilt, stretch, outline, add a glow, or add a gradient to the wordmark. Only stickers tilt.
- Use the lime-3 dark version on a light ground. The 3 vanishes (1.0:1). Use the light version.
- Swap the 3 for an emoji, a flame or a 3D render. The name is not about 3D.
- Lock the wordmark up with a college crest. See Partners for co-branding.
- Put it on a busy photo without a dark scrim behind it.

## Writing the name

In sentences it’s **Bunk3d**, with a capital B and the numeral 3. Say it “bunked”. The hashtag is **#Bunk3d** and handles are **@bunk3d**. All caps (BUNK3D) appears only inside an 11px `label`, such as an eyebrow line.
