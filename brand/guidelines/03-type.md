# Typography

There are two faces, both free on Google Fonts.

- **Unbounded ExtraBold (800)** is our voice at volume. Use it for headlines, the wordmark, big numbers and slide titles. It is wide and round, so use few words: three to six per headline.
- **Inter (400–800)** is for everything people actually read: body, captions, legal lines, UI.

## The scale

| Style | Size / line | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mega` | 120 / 0.9, −5px | posters and standees, 2–3 words |
| `hero` | 72 / 0.92, −3px | post headline, landing hero |
| `title` | 40 / 1.0, −1.6px | story headline, slide title |
| `section` | 24 / 1.1, −0.8px | section and card titles |
| `number` | 48 / 1.0, −2px | real numbers: ₹, dates, counts |
| `lead` | 18 / 1.5, 500 | the line under a headline |
| `body` | 15 / 1.5, 500 | running copy |
| `sticker` | 14 / 1.2, 800 | inside stickers, lowercase |
| `meta` | 12 / 1.4, 600 | captions, credits, fine print |
| `label` | 11 / 1.3, 800, +1.4px, CAPS | eyebrows and badges, the only all-caps |

Sizes are given at 1080px artboard width for posts and stories. They are the same numbers in px for web.

## Rules

- Set headlines in sentence case or all lowercase. Never use Title Case, and never put a full stop on a one-line headline.
- Highlight one word per headline at most, in `lime` text on dark or inside a lime chip on light.
- Keep tight tracking on Unbounded. Never letterspace it open, and never set it under 20px.
- Body copy stays left-aligned and at most 60 characters wide. Centring is for stickers and one-line CTAs only.
- Use numerals for numbers (3 days, ₹800, 4th yr), and the ₹ sign with Indian grouping: ₹1,24,000.
- If Unbounded can’t load (email, SMS), fall back to Arial Black for the headline. Never use a script font or fake-handwriting font.
