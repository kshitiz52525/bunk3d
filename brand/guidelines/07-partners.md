# Colleges, clubs and brand partners

Partners make the campus feel real, but they never make Bunk3d look official when it isn’t.

## Colleges

- Use a college’s name or logo **only with written permission** from its authorised office. Without it, say “your campus” or “students in Bengaluru”.
- Never say or imply “official app of [college]” unless a signed MoU says so. Add “Not affiliated with [college]” wherever a college name appears without a partnership.
- Never put a college crest inside a sticker, next to the wordmark, or on merch.

## Clubs (co-branding)

- **Lockup:** the club logo on the left, an `ink-muted` × in the middle, the Bunk3d wordmark on the right, both at the same optical height. Clear space is the height of the 3 around the whole lockup. See the Co-brand lockup card.
- Club colours stay inside the club logo. The layout around it is still Bunk3d: ground, type and stickers.
- A club accepting a collab is not college permission. Event posters that use campus venues need the college approval line: “Approved by [office], [date]”.

## Brand partners and sponsors

- Anything paid for by a brand carries the label **Sponsored** in `label` style, top-left, on every frame and slide.
- A partner logo is never larger than the Bunk3d wordmark on our channels.
- Separate verified numbers from claimed ones, as the app does: “48 students paid (verified)” vs “2M riders (brand’s own number)”.
- A brand can’t target by personal data. Campaign copy says “students at these campuses”, never “people like you”.

## Campus ambassadors

Ambassadors use the kit (post, story and poster templates) and change only the event details and their own photo. They don’t change colours, fonts or the logo, and they don’t write new claims about money or safety.
