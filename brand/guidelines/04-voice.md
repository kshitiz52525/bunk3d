# Voice and copy

We write like the friend in your batch who always knows what’s going on: quick, funny when it lands, and never trying too hard. When the topic is money, safety or rules, the jokes stop and the facts get exact.

## How we sound

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| campus-native: canteen, PG, internals, PYQs, OD, fest | brand-young: “fam”, “lit”, “yaas” in every line |
| lowercase in stickers, sentence case elsewhere | SHOUTY ALL CAPS, Title Case Headlines |
| specific: “Block A samosa went from ₹15 to ₹20” | vague: “Stay connected with your campus community!” |
| plain about money: “₹800 fixed. Paid after approval.” | hype about money: “Earn BIG from your phone!!” |
| inclusive Hinglish where it’s natural, one phrase per post at most | forced slang or regional jokes about any group |

## Writing rules

- **Headlines:** 3 to 6 words, a verb if possible: “spill the tea”, “get the bag”, “find your squad”.
- **Body:** one idea per post. Say what it is, who it’s for, and what to tap.
- **CTAs:** verb first and specific: “Download Bunk3d”, “Join with your college email”, “See this week’s gigs”. Never “Click here” or “Learn more”.
- **Emoji:** none in graphics, logos or stickers. In captions, use one emoji at most and never in place of a word.
- **Exclamation marks:** one per caption, at most.
- **People:** say “students”, “your batch” or “your college”. Never “users”, “Gen Z” or “youth”.
- **Colleges:** name a college only with its permission (see Partners). Otherwise say “your campus”.

## Topics with fixed wording

- **Anonymous posting:** “Post anonymously. Your name stays hidden from other students.” Never call it “untraceable”. Mods can act on serious reports.
- **Spark:** “Meet people for study, sports, events and hangouts. 18+. Not a dating app.” Never use romantic framing, couples or hearts.
- **Earnings:** “Paid gigs from clubs and verified brands. Every gig shows what you take home before you apply. Your first ₹1,000 is fee-free.” When the fee comes up, state it exactly: 5% on paid gigs, 2.5% on club gigs, 0% on referral gigs, never more than ₹1,000 a gig. Lead with take-home, never the gross pay alone, and never say “no fees” or “keep 100%”. Never quote a monthly income figure.
- **Location (Radar):** “Off by default. Approximate only. You choose who sees it and for how long.”
- **Verification:** “Only verified students get in. Your college can’t see what you post.”
