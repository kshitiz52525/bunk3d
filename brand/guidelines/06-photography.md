# Photography

Our pictures look like they came off a student’s phone at the right moment: canteen tables, terraces at sunset, fest crowds, night turf, the 3 a.m. hackathon glow. Real places, real light, and not a stock model in sight.

## What to shoot

- **Places with a story:** canteens, hostel terraces, libraries, the gate at 9 a.m., the bus stop in the rain.
- **Moments, not poses:** hands on laptops, a crowd with phones up, a team mid-celebration, food from above.
- **Night and flash are welcome.** Deep blacks sit well on our dark ground.
- **Crop:** 4:5 for posts and 9:16 for stories and reels. Leave the top third calm for a headline.

## Treatment

- Keep colour natural and contrast punchy. Use no filters and no colour-grading presets, and never wash out the blacks.
- Over busy areas, put text on a dark scrim (`bg` at 0 → 85% from the bottom up), never on the raw photo.
- Frame photos with `radius-xl` corners on posters and slides, or bleed them full on stories.
- Stickers go on the photo, never over a face.

## Consent and safety (non-negotiable)

- Every identifiable student needs a signed photo release for marketing. Group photos need a release from everyone in focus.
- Don’t show a recognisable face next to anonymous-post content. It implies they wrote it.
- Don’t photograph minors. Bunk3d is 18+.
- Never use a college’s crest, gate signage or uniform as the focus without written permission.
- Don’t use stock photos of “Indian college students” or AI-generated people.

## Placeholder library

The **Photography** asset group has six stand-in scenes (terrace sunset, fest stage, turf final, canteen samosas, hackathon night, Block C in the rain). They are drawn illustrations made for layout tests. Swap them for real, released photos before anything is published.
