# Formats and templates

Build at these sizes. The type scale is written for a 1080px-wide artboard.

| Format | Size | Safe area | Template card |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram / LinkedIn feed post | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | 64px all sides | Social post |
| Story, reel cover, WhatsApp status | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | top 250px and bottom 340px clear of text | Story |
| LinkedIn / X link image | 1200 × 627 | 48px | build from Social post |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280 × 720 | right 200px clear (timestamp) | build from Poster |
| Campus poster | A3, 297 × 420 mm | 15 mm margin, 3 mm bleed | Poster |
| Noticeboard flyer | A4, 210 × 297 mm | 12 mm margin, 3 mm bleed | Poster, light theme |
| Roll-up standee | 850 × 2000 mm | keep message above 1500 mm, nothing in the bottom 300 mm | Poster, stretched |
| Slide deck | 1920 × 1080 | 96px | build from Poster |

## Every asset has

1. The wordmark (or the app icon under 96px), in the right version for its ground.
2. A headline of six words or fewer in Unbounded.
3. One lime block or sticker.
4. A clear CTA: “Download Bunk3d”, a date and place, or a link. Use a QR code on print, at least 25 mm, ink on white with a 4-module quiet zone.
5. The legal line where needed: “Sponsored”, “18+”, “Not affiliated with [college]”.

## Pre-publish checklist

- The headline is under 6 words and makes sense without the photo.
- Every ₹ figure, date and fee was checked against the live product today.
- Photo releases are on file for every identifiable face.
- College name or logo use has written permission.
- “Sponsored” is on every frame of a paid post.
- No emoji or gradients in the graphic, and no lime text on a light ground.
