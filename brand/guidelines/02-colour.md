# Colour

Bunk3d is ink and lime first, with violet as the friend standing behind it. Hot pink is a one-off shout. Everything else is a state colour and stays out of headlines.

## Proportions on a typical asset

| Share | Token | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~60% | `bg` / `surface` | the ground; lets photos and lime glow |
| ~25% | `lime` | the biggest colour block: headline sticker, CTA, a slab behind the photo |
| ~10% | `violet` | the hard shadow, a second sticker outline, anon or Hustle content |
| ≤5% | `hot` | one “new”, “live” or “last day” mark, never two |

A post with no lime on it isn’t a Bunk3d post.

## Pairs that work (contrast checked)

- `ink` on `bg` dark is 17.7:1. `ink-muted` on `bg` dark is 7.0:1.
- `on-lime` (#0D0D0F) on `lime` is 16.8:1. This is the CTA and sticker pair.
- `lime` as text on `bg` dark is 16.8:1. Use it for one highlighted word in a headline.
- `violet` as text on `bg` dark is 8.5:1, and `anon-ink` on `anon-tile` is 8.9:1.
- On light: `ink` on `bg` is 16.7:1, `ink-muted` is 5.4:1, and `violet` (light) is 5.0:1.

## Pairs that fail. Don’t use them for text.

- **Lime on any light ground:** 1.0:1. On light, lime is a fill with `on-lime` text on top.
- **White on lime:** fails. Always use `on-lime`.
- **Light-theme `hot`, `success`, `warning`:** 4.1, 3.9 and 3.8:1 on light `bg`. Use them as fills, or for text 24px and up only.

## The two modes

- **Social side** (Tea, Study, Poppin’, Crew, Discover): lime leads.
- **The Hustle** (gigs, My Bag, Swap): `violet` leads, with lime as the shadow. This is the same flip the app makes when you swipe up on your photo. Use it for any earning or gig campaign, so students learn that violet means the money side.

## Print

Lime is brighter than CMYK can print, so the on-screen colour will dull on paper. For standees and posters, ask your printer for a fluorescent or spot match to #D4FF3A and approve a printed proof before the run. If you print in CMYK, use the light theme and treat lime as a fill under ink type. The type then carries the message even if the lime prints flat.

## Not ours

Purple-to-blue gradients, neon glows, rainbow confetti, pastel washes, and red as a headline colour.
