Bunk3d takes a small student fee from what you earn — shown on every gig before you apply, locked when you apply, and capped at ₹1,000 a gig. 18% GST is charged on top of the fee (not on your pay) and shown on every gig. It’s only taken from money actually paid: nothing on amounts under dispute, and refunded if a brand’s payment is reversed. Brands also pay Bunk3d their own 4–7% on top of your pay.
