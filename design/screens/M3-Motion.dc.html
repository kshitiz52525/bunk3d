Scroll & photo motion

Fast and springy, never floaty. Only transform and opacity animate, so scrolling stays at 60fps on a ₹10k Android. Try them live in the Tea feed (T1) and T9–T11.

1 · Header hide / peek
Scroll down > 150px: header + stories slide up 150px. Scroll up 6px: top bar peeks back (stories stay hidden until top). 280ms snap.
2 · Card reveal
Scroll-linked, not timed: each card rises 36px, scales .96→1 and fades in across its first 55% of entry. Never replays on scroll back.
3 · Photo parallax
Photo sits at 114% inside its frame and drifts ±6% opposite to scroll. Off in battery saver and reduced motion.
4 · Tab bar + Post button
Tab bar drops away with the header. “Post a pic” pill shrinks to a 56px square after 40px of scroll, and drops to 28px when the tab bar hides.

5 · Pull to refresh
The “3” from the wordmark scales in as you pull, spins once at the 72px threshold (haptic tick), then the list springs back.

6 · Double-tap upvote
Lime arrow sticker overshoots to 125%, settles, floats up 30px and fades: 800ms. The vote pill pops to 130% at the same time.
7 · Carousel snap
Native scroll-snap, one photo per swipe. The active dot stretches to 18px lime in 220ms. Counter “2 / 3” in the post header.
8 · Open + drag-to-close viewer
The photo grows from its feed spot to full screen while black fades in (340ms). Dragging down shrinks it and fades the black; let go after 120px to close.
9 · Story progress
5s per photo, linear bar, slow 8% zoom (Ken Burns) on photos. Hold to pause. The next person’s stories rotate in like a 24° cube turn.

11 · Sticker pop + wide photos
Stickers scale .6 → 1.08 → 1 one after another (120ms apart). Photos that aren’t 9:16 sit on a blurred, darkened copy of themselves — never black bars.
Rules for engineering
• Animate transform and opacity only. Header blur is static, never animated.
• Scroll-linked effects follow the finger 1:1. Only the snaps use easing.
• Reduced motion: no parallax, reveal or Ken Burns; fades only, at 120ms.
• Feed photos: 4:5 tallest, 1.91:1 widest; the viewer always shows the original.
• Load a 1080px photo, show a 40px blurred thumbnail until it arrives.
